Been reading and listening to podcasts and articles about software engineering in the age of AI recently. I'm probably biased, but I agree with the sentiment that with AI, it remains (and maybe becomes more) important to develop and build strong engineering fundamentals and discipline. I believe being a good engineer involves understanding the systems that you are building or have built, and this entails understanding both the code that you are shipping and the context within which the product of the code operates.

The stuff that I've been reading:
- [The third golden age of software engineering](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-third-golden-age-of-software)
- [AI demands more engineering discipline. Not less](https://charitydotwtf.substack.com/p/ai-demands-more-engineering-discipline)
